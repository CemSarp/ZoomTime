# ZoomTime 0.4.0 — Private group calls (prerelease)

Private calls now support **two to five named people** on Apple Silicon Macs running macOS 14 or newer. This is a test prerelease; real multi-Mac/network and long-session acceptance remains pending.

**Known issue: there is still a screen-share/screen-capture permission bug.** macOS may repeatedly request Screen Recording permission or refuse capture even after you enable it. This release does not claim to fix that bug. The screen-capture feature currently saves local diagnostics only; sharing a screen with call participants and protected-video capture are not implemented. Camera/microphone calls use their separate permissions.

## Changes

- One private group with up to five named members, individual encrypted pairing credentials and host-managed names/access.
- Choose who to call; every recipient accepts explicitly. Joined participants can invite other paired members during a call.
- One camera/microphone capture per Mac, with independent encrypted WebRTC links to every other participant. A failed link does not interrupt healthy links.
- Stable gallery, pin/unpin, active-speaker highlighting and compact participant controls in Normal and Mini modes.
- Leaving removes only your media while at least two participants remain. The host can leave the conversation while keeping the app open to coordinate the call; host quit, sleep or prolonged loss ends it.
- Encrypted legacy pairing migration retains credentials and an owner-only copy of the original ciphertext. All members must upgrade together to protocol v2; older running builds cannot join.
- Native screen-capture diagnostics and permission-recovery guidance are included, with the unresolved permission issue above.
- Apple Silicon/arm64 only. Intel/Rosetta support is retired; historical releases remain available.

**Inside ZoomTime:** Settings → Check for updates → Download update. Version0.3.4 offers **Install and restart**, preserving the previous app as a backup. Finish calls/device checks/screen capture first; a signaling host must wait until the group call ends. Versions0.3.2/0.3.3 may finish in Finder: quit the app, extract the ZIP and replace ZoomTime.app in Applications. Version0.2.0 uses the direct ZIP. Local0.3.4 experimental previews can also fetch the higher version.

No Node/Xcode or GitHub account is needed. Private builds are ad-hoc signed and unnotarized: follow normal per-app macOS, Keychain and media approvals. Never disable Gatekeeper or strip quarantine. Pairing/preferences stay in your profile; changed signatures may require fresh consent. You do not need to reset pairing merely to upgrade.

Each person uses their own Tailscale identity. Every participating pair needs connectivity, including guest-to-guest links; reaching only the host is insufficient. No STUN/TURN, public hosting, automatic background updates, recording or analytics is added.

## Evidence and remaining limits

Local tests cover source/socket/lifecycle behavior, real generated-media WebRTC calls with2/3/4/5 clients, all ten bidirectional links at capacity, invitation/departure/rejoin, isolated recovery, pinning without stopping audio/video, and packaged controls/security at both Mini sizes. Generated media on one Mac does not establish real network or hardware performance. The release build manifest identifies the exact committed source and artifact digest.

**Still pending:** actual2–5-Mac routes, hardware acoustics/device recovery, screen permission recovery, full-screen players/Spaces on all Macs, downloaded install/restart and migration approvals, and60-minute/three-hour five-person CPU/memory/bandwidth/thermal measurements. Keep this build a prerelease while those gates remain open.

**Local previews named ZoomTime Experimental.app:** Check/Download works, but the current installer accepts only ZoomTime.app in /Applications or ~/Applications. Use Show verified download and Finder for these renamed previews; do not reset pairing or disable security.
