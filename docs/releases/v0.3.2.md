Requires **macOS 14 or newer**. Download the app ZIP for your processor, not GitHub's Source code archive. Check its SHA-256, quit ZoomTime, extract the ZIP and drag ZoomTime.app into Applications; choose Replace if updating. Pairing and preferences live outside the app bundle. No Node or Xcode is needed to run it.

This free private build is ad-hoc signed and unnotarized. Follow [Apple's per-app approval procedure](https://support.apple.com/en-gb/102445) for a build you trust. Keychain, camera/microphone or Downloads prompts may appear after replacing the app. Never disable Gatekeeper or remove quarantine to make it open.

## Camera and call settings

- Choose **360p**, **720p** (default) or **1080p** capture quality. Live changes preserve the call; actual quality depends on your camera and connection.
- **Mirror my preview** flips only your own local view, not the video your partner receives.
- Optionally show your own camera in mini mode. Resize the self-preview with its corner or the Settings slider; the same relative size applies in both modes.
- Settings follows system light/dark appearance, with clearer controls and compact layouts. Reset Pairing is red, Settings has a visible button surface, and pairing actions wrap without overlapping.

## Camera and microphone checks

In **Settings → Camera & microphone check**, explicitly start a local preview and watch the microphone level. Turn on **Listen to my microphone** to hear yourself through your current output device; use headphones to prevent feedback. Listening starts off and is not remembered. Nothing is recorded or sent.

Stop, Done/Escape, switching to mini mode, starting/receiving a call, sleep, reload, quit or the two-minute limit releases check devices. A check cannot authorize a call and cannot run during an active call. Late capture and monitoring results cannot revive a cancelled check.

## Easier Tailscale setup

**Host on this Mac** opens the installed Tailscale app, discovers the current private hostname and configures only ZoomTime's free private Serve port. You no longer need to copy an endpoint from Terminal. Existing conflicting services are preserved. **Paste partner's invitation** opens/connects Tailscale before pairing.

Both Macs still need Tailscale, separate user accounts in the same private network and both apps open. Initial sign-in, VPN/HTTPS approval and invitations remain explicit user actions. ZoomTime does not send invitations or change unrelated tailnet services.

## Window fixes

Full-screen → mini and full-screen → Dock minimization now wait for macOS to leave full screen. Normal bounds are preserved. Escape exits full screen/restores mini; Command-M minimizes to Dock; Command-Shift-M toggles mini. The same window, renderer and call survive mode changes.

## Check for Updates

Use **ZoomTime → Check for Updates…** or **Settings → App updates**. The app selects a newer matching Mac ZIP from CemSarp/ZoomTime on GitHub, labels prereleases, downloads over verified HTTPS, checks size/SHA-256 and retains macOS quarantine. Then choose **Show verified download in Finder**, quit the app, extract and replace it in Applications.

There are no startup/background checks, silent replacements, forced restarts or downgrades. Install 0.3.2 manually once to get these controls; 0.2.0 has no updater. GitHub checksums verify integrity against the release metadata, not an independent notarized publisher identity.
